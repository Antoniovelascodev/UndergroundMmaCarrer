**Underground MMA Career** is a tactical mixed martial arts career and combat simulation game developed in **Flutter**. Built as an advanced multiplatform application project, it heavily focuses on Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) principles in **Dart**, dynamic attribute management, and immersive sports simulation mechanics. 🚀

---

## 🎮 Game Concept & Career Progression

The game takes players on an immersive journey through three distinct professional tiers:

1. **The Underground / Regional Circuit:** Start fighting in local gyms and improvised cages for small purses. Focus strictly on grinding your basic stats (*Striking*, *Grappling*, *Stamina*) to catch the eye of regional scouts.
2. **National Contender:** Sign with major regional promotions, handle media obligations, deal with pre-fight weight cut mechanics, and climb the official weight-class rankings.
3. **World Championship:** Fight for global championship belts in sold-out arenas against elite opponents with near-maxed attributes, where injury management and training camp resting strategies are vital.

---

## ✨ Key Features (Planned & In Development)

- 📊 **Fighter Dashboard & Stats:** Real-time tracking of attributes (`Striking`, `Grappling`, `Stamina`, `Chin`), wins/losses records, and overall fame.
- 🏋️ **Training Camp System:** Choose how to spend your weekly preparation—focusing on technical skills or physical conditioning.
- 🥊 **Simulated Fight Engine:** Algorithmic combat resolution combining fighter attributes, tactics, and procedural RNG factors.
- 📱 **Multiplatform UI:** Designed to run seamlessly across Android, iOS, Desktop, and Web via Flutter.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Framework:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/)
- **Language:** [Dart](https://dart.dev/)
- **Architecture:** Object-Oriented Design, modular models (`Fighter`, `FightEngine`), and clean state management.
